# Adjusted 3-Day Strength Routines - L2-L3 Friendly

Updated: 2026-08-28

These routines are adjusted for an L2-L3 lumbar disc herniation history. They are not medical treatment. Stop any movement that causes sharp back pain, radiating leg pain, numbness, tingling, or weakness, and use a doctor/physio plan as the source of truth.

Core rules:

1. Keep a neutral lumbar spine.
2. Avoid loaded spinal flexion, twisting, and heavy axial loading.
3. Prefer machines, cables, chest-supported rows, and seated work with back support.
4. Rest 5 minutes on the heaviest main sets, 4 minutes on compound/machine work, and 3 minutes on isolation/core work. Use at least 3 minutes when changing exercises.
5. Use 1-3 reps in reserve. No grinders.

Rest map:

- 5 min: heavy Bench Press work sets and Sled 45 Degree Leg Press work sets.
- 4 min: incline press, seated shoulder press, lat pulldown, supported rows, seated row, and secondary leg press/supported split squat.
- 3 min: lateral raise, fly/pec deck, triceps, curls, leg curls/extensions, calves, Pallof press, dead bug, bird dog, side plank, warm-up and mobility work.

## DAY A - PUSH

Goal: keep bench progress while reducing spine stress and adding shoulders/core safely.

1. Bench Press
   - Warm-up: 20 kg x 20-30, 50 kg x 10-15, 90 kg x 5
   - Work: 3 x 5-8 at 120-130 kg
   - Keep feet planted, glutes on bench, no aggressive arch.
   - Progress only when all work sets are clean and symptom-free.

2. Incline Machine Press or Incline Bench Press
   - 3 x 8-12 at 65-75 kg
   - Prefer machine if low back feels sensitive.

3. Lever Seated Shoulder Press With Back Support
   - 3 x 8-12 at 45-50 kg
   - Back supported, no standing overhead press.

4. Cable One Arm Lateral Raise
   - 3 x 12-20 each side
   - Light and strict.

5. Cable Standing Fly or Pec Deck
   - 2-3 x 10-15
   - If standing cable fly irritates the back, use pec deck.

6. Triceps Pushdown
   - 3 x 10-15 at 70-80 kg
   - Ribs down, no leaning hard into the cable.

7. Pallof Press
   - 3 x 10-15 each side
   - Anti-rotation core instead of loaded twisting/crunching.

Remove from regular DAY A:

- Standing overhead press
- Heavy dips after bench
- Extra decline/close-grip bench every week
- Crunch-heavy core if it causes lumbar flexion symptoms

## DAY B - PULL

Goal: keep back volume, rebuild rows, and avoid unsupported hip-hinge loading.

1. Cable Wide-Grip Lat Pulldown
   - Warm-up: 55 kg x 15-20
   - Work: 3 x 8-12 at 70-77 kg
   - Stay tall, no swinging or lumbar extension.

2. Chest-Supported Row or Lever Seated Row
   - 4 x 8-12 at 60-70 kg
   - Prefer chest-supported if available.

3. Cable Seated Row With V Bar
   - 3 x 8-12 at 60-70 kg
   - Neutral spine, no rocking.

4. Cable Standing Face Pull With Rope
   - 3 x 12-20
   - Light, strict, shoulder blades controlled.

5. Barbell Curl or Preacher Curl
   - 3 x 8-12
   - Prefer preacher/machine curl if standing curls make you lean back.

6. Concentration Curl or Dumbbell Preacher Curl
   - 2 x 10-15

7. Dead Bug
   - 3 x 8-12 each side
   - Slow, low back controlled against the floor.

Remove from regular DAY B:

- Barbell row
- Heavy unsupported dumbbell row
- Deadlift/RDL/good morning
- Multiple pulldown variants in the same session
- More than 5 direct biceps sets

## DAY C - LEGS + CORE

Goal: train legs hard without provoking L2-L3 symptoms.

1. Recumbent Bike or Easy Walk Warm-up
   - 5-8 minutes

2. Full Squat Mobility Only
   - 2 x 15-25 at 20 kg or bodyweight
   - Do not progress load for now.
   - Skip if symptoms appear.

3. Sled 45 Degree Leg Press
   - 3-4 x 8-12 at 150-180 kg
   - Do not go deeper than you can keep pelvis and low back fixed to the pad.
   - No lockout bouncing. No max attempts.

4. Lever Lying Leg Curl
   - 3 x 8-12 at 50-60 kg

5. Lever Leg Extension
   - 3 x 10-15
   - Controlled tempo.

6. Lever Seated Leg Press or Supported Split Squat
   - 2-3 x 10-15
   - Use supported split squat only if balance and back position stay clean.

7. Single Leg Calf Raise Off Step
   - 4 x 10-20 each side

8. Bird Dog
   - 3 x 8-12 each side

9. Side Plank
   - 3 x 20-45 sec each side

Remove from regular DAY C:

- Loaded squat progression
- Deadlift/RDL/good morning
- Lever seated twist
- Heavy axial loading
- Deep leg press range that rounds the low back
- Random arm/chest extras

## Weekly Structure

Monday: DAY A - PUSH

Wednesday: DAY B - PULL

Friday: DAY C - LEGS + CORE

If a day is missed, continue with the next routine in order. Do not skip ahead.

## Progression Rules

1. Progress only if the session and the next morning are symptom-free.
2. Add reps before weight.
3. Add weight in small jumps only after all sets hit the top of the rep range with clean form.
4. Deload immediately if back symptoms increase for more than 24 hours after training.
5. Track these main lifts:
   - Bench Press
   - Cable Wide-Grip Lat Pulldown
   - Chest-Supported Row or Lever Seated Row
   - Sled 45 Degree Leg Press
6. Weekly targets:
   - Chest: 8-10 hard sets
   - Back: 12-14 hard sets
   - Legs: 10-14 hard sets
   - Shoulders: 8-10 hard sets
   - Spine-friendly core: 6-9 hard sets
   - Direct arms: 5-8 hard sets
